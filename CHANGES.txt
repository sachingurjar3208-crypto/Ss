STYLE 2 CARD + /cardstats  -  what changed
==========================================

NEW FILES
  card_stats.py     Stat sets per role (BAT / BOWL / AR / WK) in one dict, STAT_SETS.
                    card_stat_rows(card), card_stat_details(card), allowed_extra_keys(role).
                    Unset extra stats are derived from bat / bowl / ovr with a small fixed
                    variation (-4..+4), so old cards never break.
  card_style2.py    The Style 2 renderer: build_card_style2(card_row, fg_image) -> 1037x1517 PNG.
                    Static parts are cached once; one card renders in about 0.5 s.
  cardstats_cog.py  The admin-only /cardstats command.

CHANGED FILES
  card_db.py        + columns card_style, extra_stats (auto-migrate on start)
                    + set_card_style(), set_extra_stats(), get_extra_stats()
                    + import json
  card_image.py     generate_card_image(): if card_style == "style2" it uses card_style2.
                    Everything else is unchanged, so old cards look exactly the same.
  main.py           "cardstats_cog" added to the cog list.

HOW TO USE
  /cardstats player:Virat Kohli pwr:94 run:99 tim:95 clu:97 fie:95
     - saves the stats, switches the card to Style 2 and posts the new card
     - stats that do not belong to the role are ignored, with a note
     - style:Classic (old design) switches a card back
  /refreshcards redraws every card, Style 2 cards included.

Copy these 6 files over your project folder (same names) and restart the bot.
No new libraries needed. /cardmaker, /editcard and the match engine are untouched.

FIELD PLACEMENT v2 - 11 fielders, formations, catch wheel
==========================================================

WHAT CHANGED
  field_logic.py    Every legal field has exactly 11 fielders in 11 zones:
                    8 outfield (outside 30 yd: UL U UR L R DL D DR) and
                    3 inner (inside 30 yd: IL IS IR). 10 formation presets
                    (FORMATIONS). Powerplay: max 2 outside. Open overs: max 5.
                    Inner fielders stop dots/singles/threes and take short
                    catches. loft_power(bat) decides how far a batter can
                    clear the rope with a lofted ball that is not caught.
  logic.py          calculate_outcome(..., loft_power=None). Low bat = few
                    sixes, more wickets when going after it. High bat = sixes.
  wagon_wheel.py    All 11 fielders drawn and numbered 1-11.
                    New catch_wheel_png(): RED curved line from the batter to
                    the fielder who took a lofted catch, ending in OUT.
  commentary.py     No emoji. Short 2-3 line lines, symbols (★ » ▲) only.
                    New catch lines (each contains CAUGHT so the dismissal is
                    recorded as a catch). "at <zone>" is named.
  game.py           field_positions is now {zone_key: count}; set_field() takes it.
  views.py          Field prompt is shown at the START of an innings too: after
                    the first bowler is picked, the captain sets the field for
                    over 1 (powerplay cap applies), then the first delivery.
                    Field prompt: formation dropdown + Customise + Confirm.
                    Editor: 11 zone buttons laid out like the ground, Add/Remove
                    mode, Reset, Confirm (only when all 11 are placed).
  squad_cog.py      csfield shows the new field layout.

RULES
  - Every field must place exactly 11 players.
  - Outside the 30-yard circle: at most 2 (powerplay) or 5 (other overs).
  - Inside the circle: no cap, max 6 in any one inner zone.

NOT CHANGED
  - Batting UI, DRS, bowler selection, playstyles other than the loft rules.
  - Per-player fielder stats (still Phase 2, not built).
